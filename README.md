# Huangqihai Civilization · 黄旗海文明

A systems-level study of the Huangqihai Basin and the Miaozigou Neolithic site as a window onto northern Chinese frontier civilization. Bilingual (EN / 中文), dark/light, archaeological-ecological aesthetic with Italiana + Lora + Space Mono on dark slate-green with lake-cyan, sage, ochre, and bone accents. Ten modules: hero map of the closed lake basin showing Huangqihai, Miaozigou, neighbouring settlements, three migration corridors, and four ecological zones (steppe, loess piedmont, forest, lake); four basin-ecology cards (hydrology, tectonics, vegetation, why-basins); Miaozigou site module with eight inline-SVG artifact cards (painted pottery, grinding slab, semi-subterranean house, burial assemblage, bone awl, faunal mix, jade-like ornaments, hearth) plus four interpretive cards (settlement structure, subsistence, cemetery, end-of-occupation); seven-row Yellow-River-core / Northern-frontier / Southern-rice comparison table; Holocene lake-level chart with Mid-Holocene optimum and 4.2 ka aridification overlay; four Neolithic-network cards (Yangshao, Hongshan, Longshan, Miaozigou complex); four steppe-agriculture cards on coupling, frontier innovation, conflict, and hybridity; four ritual cards on burial, pottery symbolism, ancestor cult, and sacred landscape; basin civilization simulator (5 climate regimes × 4 levers × 6 outcome dimensions); four long-term-history cards (Bronze, Han-Wei, Ming-Qing, modern); four frontier-theory cards inverting center-radiating models; AI Archaeologist with five canned multi-paragraph responses and free-text heuristic fallback; explicit guardrails section refusing ethnic-ancestry claims, center-periphery erasure, environmental determinism, and archaeology-by-anecdote.

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript. No build step. Bilingual via `[data-lang]` + CSS attribute selectors. Light/dark via `[data-theme]`. Italiana display, Lora body, Space Mono specimen. All diagrams inline SVG. JS uses template literals throughout — verified with `node --check`.
